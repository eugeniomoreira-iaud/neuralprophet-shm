# Design · The monitoring system and its record — data exploration rebuilt

**Study:** `studies/data_exploration/`
**Date:** 15 August 2026
**Status:** design approved, implementation not started

---

## 1. What this study is for

`studies/data_exploration/` becomes the project's documentation of the monitoring system
installed on the **Mura Urbiche di Gubbio**, the medieval urban walls of Gubbio, Umbria.
It answers three questions, in this order: what the instrumentation is, how the structure
is monitored and how the acquisition writes its record, and what data that record actually
contains. Diagnostics follow from the description rather than replacing it.

The study is deliberately the first document a reader of this project should open. Every
other study assumes the instrumentation, the file format, the compensation and the shape
of the archive; this one states them.

This design covers the first instalment. The user will extend the report from this
baseline.

### 1.1 What this replaces

The existing study is a generic first look at one station's frame: `df.info()`,
`df.describe()`, a missing-percentage bar chart, one series plot, a histogram grid and a
correlation heat map. It is discarded in full.

Three of its properties make it unsuitable as the project's data document. It pools the
two instrument eras, so its mean and standard deviation of `inc_comp` average two
unrelated baselines across the 21 February 2025 replacement. It reports missingness as one
percentage per column, which says nothing about whether the absence is one long outage or
scattered hours. And it sees one station, while the archive holds three and the fact that
two of them stopped recording years before the changeover is the most consequential thing
about the record's shape.

### 1.2 What is out of scope for this instalment

Not included, and each is a natural later addition: the gap anatomy and the
missingness-mechanism test of the unified report's section 4; solar radiation and wall
temperature, which exist only in the current era; the completeness calendar and the
per-channel availability timeline; any correlation, lag or spectral analysis.

## 3. Approach

The notebook imports `ud_lib` from the sibling `unified_dataset` study and adds no library
of its own. Statistics that `ud_lib` already owns are called, not reimplemented, so the
two studies cannot drift apart in what they measure. The notebook contributes
orchestration, two small aggregations that `ud_lib` does not provide, and the figures.

Alternatives rejected: a thin `de_lib.py` supplying the missing plotters, which buys
conformity with the module standard at the cost of a file holding only plotting code; and
a self-contained `de_lib.py`, which buys independence at the cost of two implementations
of the same quantity that can silently disagree.

## 4. Report structure

`report/data_exploration_report.tex`, rewritten. Shared preamble at
`../../_shared/reportstyle.tex`, `\graphicspath{{../outputs/}}`, figures cited without an
extension so pdflatex selects the PNG.

### §1 · The monitoring system

What the site is and what is monitored: a static structural health monitoring
installation on the medieval urban walls of Gubbio, reporting inclination of the wall
together with the environmental variables that drive it. Three measurement stations, of
which **station 02 stands at the Porta di Sant'Ubaldo**, a principal gate of the medieval
circuit, and is the station carried through the rest of the report.

Figure `DE_F00_site_map` places the three stations. **The map is supplied by the user, not
generated by the notebook.** The `.tex` carries the `\includegraphics` block commented out
with a visible note, so the file can be dropped into `outputs/` later without editing the
prose.

Table: station inventory — station identifier, acquisition block, columns occupied in the
raw file, eras present, and a location note. Static, from `docs/raw-data-format.md` §3.3.

### §2 · The instrumentation

Each legacy acquisition unit reports four channels: supply voltage, air temperature,
relative humidity and inclination. The package installed at station 02 on 21 February 2025
is a physical reinstallation that reports the same four and adds two: incident solar
radiation and wall surface temperature. Its inclinometer shares no baseline with the unit
it replaced.

Table: channel dictionary — file symbol, quantity, unit, eras in which it exists, sentinel
value, and a note. Static. Observed ranges are deliberately **not** in this table; they
are measured per era in §6 and would otherwise be stated twice.

The precedent is `pc_lib.write_data_dictionary`, which produced
`docs/proxy-data-dictionary.md` for the environmental proxies.

### §3 · How the record is written

Daily plain-text files named `GUBBIO_YYYYMMDD.adc`, tab separated, no header row, date as
`DD/MM/YY` and time as `HH:MM:SS` in the first two fields, nominal sampling every
20 minutes for 72 records on a complete day.

Table: physical layout, from `docs/raw-data-format.md` §2.

**§3.1 The two column layouts.** One table for the 14-column legacy era, one for the
20-column current era, each listing field number, symbol and unit. The current-era table
records that fields 3–14 retain the three legacy blocks and are written as identical zero
on every record, because no legacy station reports after the changeover.

**§3.2 Sentinels and conventions.** Table of value, affected channels, meaning and the
action a loader must take: `0.000` as the missing-data sentinel in `Batt`, `Tair`, `RH`
and `I` but a legitimate night reading in `n_SR`; `-55.0` as the wall-temperature
probe-failure sentinel; `8191.875` as the solar-radiation wrap-around; the decimal
separator that varies within a single file and must be normalised per field.

**§3.3 From the raw grid to the analysis grid.** One paragraph: the 20-minute records are
aggregated to the hourly grid used by every study, absent slots are carried as `NaN`
rather than dropped, and the unified dataset marks provenance so that a filled value can
never be mistaken for a reading.

### §4 · Thermal compensation

The correction, stated as the manufacturer's prescribed treatment of the
temperature-induced measurement bias in the inclinometer:

```
I_comp(t) = I(t) − (T(t) − T_ref) · k · 1000,    k = 0.005  (5 mdeg per °C)
```

with `T` the station's own on-board air temperature. The section states the coefficient's
provenance as the manufacturer's specification, describes `T_ref` and the anchoring, and
repeats the interpretive rule that governs every level in the report: the absolute level
of the inclinometer carries no structural information, only its changes do.

Table: the compensation parameters and their values.

**Every inclination series shown in this report is compensated.** The raw channel is not
plotted.

### §5 · Coverage of the inclination record

Figure `DE_F01`: daily availability of `inc` for all three stations across the whole
archive, one strip per station, **with the 21 February 2025 changeover marked on every
panel**. This is the figure that shows the network narrowing from three instrumented
locations to one, in stages rather than at a single moment.

Table: station classification — eras, first and last reading, span in days, observed days,
coverage of span, coverage of archive. From `ud.classify_stations`.

Table: yearly coverage — year by station, observed fraction of the inclinometer. The
staged failure readable as numbers rather than only from the strip figure.

### §6 · Station 02, variable by variable

Why station 02: it is the best-covered record in the archive and the only one spanning
both eras, and it stands at the Porta di Sant'Ubaldo.

Table: per-era summary statistics for `inc_comp`, `tair`, `rh` and `batt` — count, mean,
standard deviation, minimum, median, maximum, split legacy against current. This replaces
the pooled `describe()` of the old study, which averaged two unrelated inclinometer
baselines into one meaningless mean.

**§6.1 Inclination.** Figure `DE_F02`, two panels. The upper panel is `inc_comp` across
the whole archive with the changeover marked and **the two comparison-window means drawn
as horizontal levels**, one on each side — the `mean_before` and `mean_after` returned by
the offset estimate, whose difference is the raw step the procedure starts from. The lower
panel is `inc_comp_joined`, the same series with the estimated offset removed.

The prose states the level disagreement and then explains how the offset is obtained: the
mean of the last 30 days of legacy readings is compared with the mean of the first 30 days
of current-era readings; the two windows sit at different points of the annual cycle, so a
harmonic model fitted to the legacy era is subtracted first and what survives is
attributed to the instrument. The diagnostic reported beside the offset is the ratio of
diurnal amplitudes either side of the changeover, which compares the instruments' gain
rather than their zero.

Table: the offset estimate and its diagnostics — interruption, raw step, seasonal part,
attributed offset, diurnal amplitude each side, gain ratio. From `ud.estimate_era_offset`.

**§6.2 Air temperature.** Figure `DE_F03`, line plot, changeover marked.

**§6.3 Relative humidity.** Figure `DE_F04`, line plot, changeover marked.

**§6.4 Battery.** Figure `DE_F05`, line plot, changeover marked. The supply voltage is
not a structural quantity; it is the acquisition system's own health, and it is shown
because it is the channel that explains the outages.

### §7 · Caveats

The compensation is instantaneous and linear, with no thermal lag term. The joined series
reconciles the levels of the two instruments and not their scales, since the diurnal
amplitude differs across the changeover. One caveat line records that the *raw* channel
slopes positively against air temperature while the site's structural convention expects
negative, and points to `docs/raw-data-format.md` §7.5 — that contradiction is independent
of the coefficient's provenance and is not resolved here.

### §8 · Artefact inventory

The table in section 5.2 below, plus the reproduction commands.

## 5. Notebook and artefacts

### 5.1 Notebook

`data_exploration_study.py`, `py:percent`, paired with the `.ipynb` by jupytext. The `.py`
is the file that is edited; the user syncs the notebook.

**Title cell** — heading, one paragraph on the study's role, numbered list of steps.

**Imports and parameters** — `sys.path` injection for `'.'`, `'../..'` and
`'../unified_dataset'`; `import ud_lib as ud`; `DATA_DIR = '../../data/interim/unified'`,
`OUTPUT_DIR = 'outputs'`, `STATION = ud.TARGET_STATION`, `STATIONS = ud.STATIONS`,
`CHANNELS = ['inc_comp', 'tair', 'rh', 'batt']`, `OFFSET_WINDOW_DAYS = 30`;
`ud.set_context('notebook')`. The preceding markdown cell documents every parameter, per
the notebook standard in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Step 1 · Read the unified dataset.** Load all three frames. Print the target station's
extent, its columns and the observed fraction of `inc_comp` and `inc_comp_joined`.

**Step 2 · Coverage of the inclination record.**
`ud.classify_stations(frames)` writes `DE_01`. A three-line aggregation,
`df['inc'].notna().resample('1YS').mean()` per station, writes `DE_02`; `ud_lib` provides
monthly completeness only. The figure calls `ud.plot_station_completeness` **without a
save path**, adds a changeover line to each returned axis, and saves PNG and SVG itself —
so the marked figure reuses the shared plotter rather than reimplementing it.

**Step 3 · The instrument changeover.**
`ud.estimate_era_offset(df, window_days=OFFSET_WINDOW_DAYS, deseason=True,
target='inc_comp')` writes `DE_04` and prints the interruption, the raw step, the seasonal
part, the attributed offset, both diurnal amplitudes and the gain ratio. The returned
`mean_before` and `mean_after` are the levels drawn in Step 4's inclination figure.

**Step 4 · Station 02, variable by variable.**
A `groupby('era')` summary over `CHANNELS` writes `DE_03`. Then the inclination figure
`DE_F02` with its two panels, followed by one line plot per remaining channel,
`DE_F03` to `DE_F05`, each with the changeover marked.

**On the `DE_T0*` bodies.** The sibling reports transcribe their numbers into the `.tex`
by hand. Three of this report's four data tables have a row count fixed by the data — nine
years of coverage, two eras times four channels — so hand transcription would have to be
redone every time the archive grows. The notebook therefore writes the row bodies and the
report `\input`s them inside its own `tabular`. The static documentation tables of report
sections 1 to 4 stay hand-written, since they describe the file format and never change
with the data.

Stale artefacts of the discarded study — `DE_01_missing_data.csv`,
`DE_02_summary_statistics.csv`, `DE_F01_missingness.pdf`, `DE_F02_series.pdf`,
`DE_F03_corr.pdf`, `DE_F04_dist.pdf` — are deleted. They are products of a study that no
longer exists and they reuse five of the new artefact IDs under different names, so
keeping them would leave `outputs/` holding two different `DE_01` and two different
`DE_F02` under one numbering scheme.

## 6. One change outside the study

`docs/raw-data-format.md` §7.4 item 3 currently reads that `COMP_COEFF = 0.005` is "a
placeholder carried from the module default and marked in the notebook as pending the
calibration sheet". That contradicts the provenance this report states. The item is
rewritten to record the coefficient as the manufacturer's specified value, while keeping
the two observations that do not depend on provenance: it has not been fitted to these
data, and there is no guarantee that the instrument installed in 2025 shares the
coefficient of the units it replaced.

Nothing else in `docs/` is touched. In particular §7.5 is left exactly as written: its
argument is that a 5 mdeg/°C correction applied to a channel whose measured slope is
+1.63 mdeg/°C flips the sign arithmetically, and that argument holds whoever specified the
coefficient.

**Flagged, not changed:** `studies/unified_dataset/report/unified_imputation_report.tex`
states in section 1.3 and again in its caveats that the coefficient "has never been
calibrated for these instruments". That wording survives the correction above, but the
report may still want revisiting for tone. Left to the user.

`ud_lib.py` is not edited. The raw archive is not read. `data_exploration_study.ipynb` is
left to the user's jupytext sync, per the pairing rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

## 7. Verification

The notebook is executed end to end with `jupyter nbconvert --execute` in
`neuralprophet_env` and must complete without error. The notebook-produced artefacts of
section 5.2 must exist in `outputs/` afterwards — four CSVs, four `.tex` bodies and five
PNG/SVG figure pairs, eighteen files; `DE_F00_site_map` is expected to be absent until
supplied.

Two numbers are checked against the unified study as a consistency test, not as a source:
the target station's observed days from `DE_01`, expected 2061.0, and the instrument
offset from `DE_04`, expected −132.476 mdeg at a 30-day window with deseasoning, matching
`studies/unified_dataset/outputs/U_03_era_offset.csv`. A disagreement means the
recomputation differs from the ingest's and must be explained before the report is
written.

Every static table in report sections 1 to 4 is checked field by field against
`docs/raw-data-format.md`. Every number in sections 5 and 6 is read from this study's own
outputs; none is carried over from another report.

The report is built with `pdflatex` twice and must produce a PDF with no unresolved
references and no missing figures. The commented-out site-map block must not trigger a
missing-file error.
